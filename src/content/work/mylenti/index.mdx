## Meaning
[Mylenti](), also known as <kbd>Myelin</kbd>, symbolizes speed and precision 
in signal transmission. Inspired by this characteristic, Mylenti provides a donation service tailored for Vietnamese streamers.

## Reasons
As the streaming industry was booming in Vietnam, 
I became curious about developing software to support donation services 
without having to share revenue with third parties. 
So, I decided to get started.
